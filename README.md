# 🌾 Village Farmer Survey Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

The Village Farmer Survey Analysis Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project focused on a single village-level farmer survey dataset. The project analyzes farmer demographics, land ownership, agricultural practices, crop cultivation trends, and estimated farming income to better understand the socio-economic condition of farmers within the village.

This dashboard helps transform raw survey records into actionable insights for rural development, agricultural planning, and community-level decision making.

---

## 📌 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Analyze the farmer population of the village.
- Understand land ownership and landlessness patterns.
- Study crop cultivation trends.
- Examine income distribution among different farmer groups.
- Highlight women-headed households and vulnerable farmer categories.
- Generate data-driven insights for local agricultural development.

## ⭐ Dashboard Highlights

### Business Problem

Village-level agricultural data is often stored in raw tabular format, making it difficult to identify important trends related to land ownership, crop selection, income distribution, and farmer demographics.

Without proper visualization, local stakeholders may struggle to understand the overall agricultural condition of the village.

### Goal of the Dashboard

The dashboard was created to:

- Provide a complete overview of village farmers.
- Identify dominant crops and farming patterns.
- Measure agricultural income performance.
- Understand land ownership structure.
- Support local planning and development decisions.

## 📈 Key Insights

### Farmer Categories

- Landless with Homestead: 255
- Small & Marginal Farmers: 185
- Women Headed Households: 69
- SC Farmers: 81
- ST Farmers: 44

### Crop Analysis

Most cultivated crops:

1. Rice
2. Soybean
3. Tur (Pigeon Pea)
4. Wheat
5. Chickpea(chana)

### Land Ownership Analysis

- Owned Land: 371 Farmers
- Leased Land: 77 Farmers
- Partly Owned / Partly Leased: 39 Farmers
- No Agricultural Land: 255 Farmers

### Income Insights

- Small and Marginal Farmers contribute the largest share of total farming income.
- Rice is the dominant crop in the village.
- A significant number of households are landless and may require alternative livelihood support.
